LOYO & H3 TECHNICAL PROTOCOL

1. H3 Spatial Indexing (Resolution 7): Each MODIS (~705 km orbit, 1 km pixel) and VIIRS (~824 km orbit, 375 m pixel, 7.1× sub-pixel resolution gain) detection is indexed onto an Uber H3 Resolution 7 geodesic hexagon (~5.16 km² global geodesic average; ~3.84 km² local effective area over the Dominican Republic), collapsing multi-pixel splits into single daily events per cell.

2. Leave-One-Year-Out Cross-Validation (LOYO, 2012–2026): Training iteratively excludes an entire overlap year out-of-sample, cutting the artificial post-2012 raw jump of ×4.52 (2,070 → 9,410) to a homogeneous ×0.92 matching independent MODIS control (×0.92), with ±2.4% provincial regime error and audited national performance of 6.29% annual error, r = 0.964 monthly correlation, and 0.008% net bias (model_national.json).

Zyxilon Explorer anchors its hybrid ensemble on H3 spatial clustering plus thermophysical FRP invariance (×1.20), isolating diurnal low-energy agricultural bias while preserving extreme fire months without under-prediction.
